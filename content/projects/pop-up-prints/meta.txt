description: Pop Up Prints is a research project exploring inflatable structures and experimental fabrication processes. Unlike traditional fabrication, the process utilizes the hot nozzle of a 3D printer, heated up to 300°C, to laminate layers of elastic TPU foil together. Developing distinct geometries and parametric patterns through an iterative design process opened up the possibility of using this 2D production process to create objects that emerge into 3D shapes when inflated.
