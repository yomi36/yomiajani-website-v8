title: Interactive Train Exhibit
year: 2022
category: Brand Experience
with: nFrontier, Artificial Rome
for: Vossloh Rolling Stock
role: Interaction & Electronics
order: 10
description: This project demonstrates the possibilities of enhancing a physical exhibit with interaction electronics and augmented reality to increase engagement and the display of additional information. The design features a train model sliced into acrylic panels, etched with technical details akin to an MRI scan, showing its complex systems in a visually engaging way. A second, overlaid layer of augmented digital reality allows exploration of engine options and detailed information, offering a seamless transition between the physical and digital realms.
