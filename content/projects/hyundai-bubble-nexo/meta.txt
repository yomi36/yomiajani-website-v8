description: In an effort to creatively showcase the sustainable benefits of the hydrogen powered Nexo vehicle from Hyundai for a commercial video production, a bubble making machine was designed and engineered that uses the clean air and water from the tailpipe to create soap bubbles that transport plant seeds into the environment.
