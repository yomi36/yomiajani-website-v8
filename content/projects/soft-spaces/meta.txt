description: Soft Spaces is the third part of an ongoing examination of air structures and inflatables. An interactive play installation shown at the kids art education center Mille Formes in Clermont-Ferrand, France and the Family Festival by Fondation Louis Vuitton in Paris. Young children are invited to experience air as a material — light, soft, and constantly changing. Guided by a mediation team, they explore a landscape of colorful inflatable forms, discovering different textures, movements, and sensations through touch and play. Together, the objects form a playful, colorful landscape that encourages curiosity and open-ended exploration.
