Set Design — 2015

Hornbach das Herrenzimmer

Set design and construction for the production of the TV commercial series Hornbach – Das Herrenzimmer. Five different stations were created in one imaginary man cave, built in four weeks using solely materials and props from the store — a forest-run simulator, a beer trough, an anti-spa, a hover chair, and a fishing oasis — including manuals to recreate everything at home.

With Neulant van Exel, Easy Does it, Heimat
